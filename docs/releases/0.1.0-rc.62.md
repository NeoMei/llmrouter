# LLMRouter v0.1.0-rc.62

## 中文

- 接入 UniHub 产品专属积分账本。每 1 元人民币对应 100 积分。模型目录公布可用操作及报价版本，请求按准入时的报价和原付款账户结算。
- 支持配置同模型、同计量项的供应商按量费率。默认按最高可比费率乘以 1.5 定价，管理员可调整倍率。已发布的明确费率优先。
- 已认证的对话、图片、视频、语音、转写、知识库和 PDF OCR 走产品积分计量。凭原幂等键查询结算回执，以及支持恢复的媒体和知识库结果。供应商结果不明的调用保留待对账状态。

## English

- Integrate UniHub product-scoped credit ledgers at 100 credits per CNY. The model catalog publishes available operations and tariff revisions. Calls settle against the price and payer fixed at admission.
- Configure comparable pay-as-you-go supplier rates per model and billing dimension. The default sale tariff applies an operator-adjustable multiplier of 1.5 to the highest comparable rate. An explicit published tariff takes precedence.
- Meter certified chat, image, video, speech, transcription, knowledge and PDF OCR operations in product credits. Use the original idempotency key to read settlement receipts and recover supported media and knowledge results. Uncertain supplier outcomes remain pending reconciliation.
